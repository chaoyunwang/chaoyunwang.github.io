## About Me

I received my Ph.D. in **Control Science and Engineering** from the [Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics](http://www.aiar.xjtu.edu.cn/#), [Xi'an Jiaotong University](http://en.xjtu.edu.cn), in September 2026, under the supervision of [Prof. Caigui Jiang](https://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/jiang/). From September 2024 to September 2025, I was a joint Ph.D. researcher with [Prof. Takeo Igarashi](https://www-ui.is.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~takeo/) at the [User Interface Research Group](https://www-ui.is.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/), [The University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/).

My research focuses on **Geometric Intelligence for Digital and Physical Worlds**, integrating **geometry, visual perception, and learning** to represent, reconstruct, and understand the 3D world. Building on my previous work in **geometric optimization and visual reconstruction**, I am interested in developing intelligent geometric representations and computational models for **3D world modeling, digital twins, and embodied intelligence**, bridging digital representations and the physical world.
